Jongrai

Project Jongrai is a draft board for Mobile Legends ranked. It tracks counters, synergies, lanes and power spikes as you pick, scores both sides in real time, and hands the finished draft to an AI coach that explains what actually decides the game.

WHAT IT DOES

Five things, one board

Everything runs locally from plain data files. No accounts, no database, no build step.

DRAFT BOARD

Pick, ban and assign lanes

Five picks and five bans a side, with flex-lane assignment and one-click undo of a misclick.

COUNTERS

Official head-to-head data

Moonton's own strong-against and synergy relations, for all 133 heroes.

CORE BUILDS

What to actually build

Three curated builds per hero, core pieces marked, plus all 104 shop items and their passives.

EDGE SCORE

Both sides, five categories

A weighted 0-100 read on counters, meta power, synergy, lockdown and comp balance.

AI COACH

A verdict once the draft ends

It stays silent while you pick, then comments on both sides and names the key fight to watch.

LIVE FROM THE ROSTER

Meta snapshot

METHOD

How the draft edge is scored

The edge is a weighted blend of five categories, each measured separately so you can see why a side is ahead instead of just that it is.

CategoryWeightMeasured from
Counters25%Pick-vs-pick matchups won, from Moonton's relations
Meta power20%Average overall win rate of the picks
Synergy15%Official synergy pairs on the same team
Lockdown15%Heroes with a heavy control rating (70+)
Comp balance25%Lane coverage, frontline, damage and role spread

The AI coach receives this readout as evidence, but is told to trust its own read when the two disagree.

READY

Start a draft

Pick a side, choose Pick or Ban, and click heroes. The coach speaks when the tenth pick lands.
