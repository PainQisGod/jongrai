About Project Jongrai

A draft board for Mobile Legends: Bang Bang. Everything runs from one CSV file on your machine.

What it is

A tool for running a ranked draft: pick and ban both sides, assign lanes, and get a live read of who won the draft and why. The aim is to explain the draft, not to predict the match.

Where the data comes from

  • Roster, tiers, win and ban rates - the shipped data/heroes.csv, patch .
  • Counters and synergies - Moonton's official in-game relations, via github.com/p3hndrx/MLBB-API (MIT).
  • Durability, offense, control and speciality tags - Moonton's in-game hero ratings.
  • Power spike (EARLY / MID / LATE) - the community dataset at github.com/Amirul-Hakimi/MLBB-DRAFT-ANALYSIS (ISC). One author's judgement, not official.
  • Item catalog (tier, cost, stats, passives) - the shipped data/items.csv, from mlbbhub.com, itself derived from Moonton and Liquipedia structural item data.
  • Core builds per hero - the shipped data/hero-builds.csv: hand-curated loadouts from mlbbhub.com, five per hero, reviewed against patch . Editorial, not official.

Ban rates under 15% are shown as blank, meaning under 15% or unknown.

The AI coach

The final verdict comes from an AI model you configure yourself. It is told the draft is patch data and is asked to judge only what is on screen.

Two rules the app enforces: it does not call the model at all while the draft is in progress, and the reply for a given draft is cached so re-rendering never re-bills you. Put your key in ai-config.txt, which is gitignored.

The coach is an opinion, not a source of truth. The counters, tiers and win rates are the data.

Running it

Requires Java 17 or newer and nothing else: no build tool, no dependencies.

./build.sh  (Windows: .\build.ps1)
java -cp out jongrai.WebServer
