This could be one of the most hyped MPL Cambodia Grand Finals ever. Both teams field five Khmer players in top form, and both are ready to put on one of the most entertaining finals the league has seen. Both also bring massive fanbases and big sponsors, and even your favorite streamers have picked a side.

## Vigor Apex: the undefeated

Vigor Apex was founded in late 2025. Their original roster members, Raa and Vincen, are still with the team, and more players have joined since. In their first MPL Cambodia season, last season, they came through the open qualifier and finished fourth. They then represented Cambodia at the Asian Games Qualifier, finishing second after wins over strong national teams such as the Philippines and Myanmar.

In Season 11 they assembled one of the strongest squads ever seen and tore through the Regular Season with a 9-0 record. They then represented Cambodia at the Asian Games in Aichi-Nagoya, but unfortunately lost 2-1 to Myanmar in the Quarterfinals. In the playoffs, they swept both TNR Esports and Team Flash KH to book their place in the Grand Final.

## Team Flash KH: the hungry

Team Flash KH was founded in 2019 under the name BURN Gaming. They entered the M1 World Championship Khmer Qualifier but didn't make it, and two years later they were one of the founding teams of MPL Cambodia Season 1. In early 2022 they teamed up with Team Flash to become Burn X Flash, and half a year later they became the first Cambodian team to import players from other countries.

They went on to win Season 3 and represented Cambodia at M4, though they didn't do well there. A season later they played at MSC 2023 on home soil and recorded the highest overall ranking for a Cambodian team, finishing fourth. In Season 6, Team Flash fully acquired BURN Gaming and the team became Team Flash KH, then went quiet. The low point came in Seasons 8 and 9, when they were eliminated from the Regular Season both times, winning just one match in each season. They then acquired the Team Max roster and earned a respectable finish a season later.

This season they started strong and sat top of the table for two weeks, but lost some momentum and ended the Regular Season in fourth place. In the playoffs they reverse swept Valhalla and took down Team RVZ before losing to VGR, then got the better of TNR to reach their first Grand Final since Season 4.

## Fans and streamers

Both teams have massive fanbases. Vigor Apex's fans, known as Vigory, have grown quickly in recent weeks, especially from the CFU Gaming community, and some fans have adopted them as their second team after See You Soon, thanks to brilliant marketing from their brand ambassador. Team Flash KH's fans, known as Flazer, include many loyal supporters who have stuck with them since the old days, along with a new generation of fans.

Two popular Cambodian streamers, M.Megamind and HunterXzKill (known as Knoy), have also picked their sides. M.Megamind is a brand ambassador for Vigor Apex, so he will definitely back them. Knoy chose Team Flash KH when they faced Vigor Apex in the Upper Bracket Final, so it's safe to say he will support them again.

## Kickoff

The Grand Final kicks off at 14:00. Come early to cheer for your team and see who lifts the trophy.
