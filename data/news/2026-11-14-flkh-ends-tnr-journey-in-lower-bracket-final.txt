## The road to the Lower Bracket Final

TNR Esports beat PRO Esports 3-2, then lost 3-0 to Vigor Apex and dropped to the Lower Bracket, where they beat Team RVZ 3-1. Meanwhile, Team Flash KH got past Valhalla 3-2 and RVZ 3-1, but lost 3-0 to VGR this afternoon and dropped into the Lower Bracket Final to meet TNR.

## Games 1 and 2

[[Lapu-Lapu]] made its debut for TNR's Mercyy in Game 1. TNR drafted for an early spike, but FLKH's late-game draft held up from the very start, and Layy took over with a Savage, the second Savage of the day.

In Game 2, FLKH's Sakzuuu debuted his [[Alice]] and carried his team to the win, despite a fierce comeback attempt from TNR.

## Game 3

No surprises in Game 3. FLKH stomped TNR to complete the sweep and earned a chance at revenge against VGR in the Grand Final.

## វគ្គទី១ និងវគ្គទី២

[[Lapu-Lapu]] ត្រូវបានជ្រើសរើសជាលើកដំបូងសម្រាប់កីឡាករ Mercyy របស់ TNR នៅវគ្គទី១។ ទោះបី TNR រៀប Draft សម្រាប់បង្កើនសម្ពាធនៅដើមវគ្គក៏ដោយ Draft ចុងវគ្គរបស់ FLKH នៅតែអាចលេងបានយ៉ាងល្អតាំងពីដើមម៉ោង ហើយ Layy បានយក Savage ដែលជា Savage ទីពីរប្រចាំថ្ងៃ។

នៅវគ្គទី២ កីឡាករ Sakzuuu បានលេង [[Alice]] ជាលើកដំបូង ហើយនាំក្រុមឈ្នះ ទោះបីជាមានការវាយបកយ៉ាងសាហាវពី TNR ក្ដី។

## វគ្គទី៣
