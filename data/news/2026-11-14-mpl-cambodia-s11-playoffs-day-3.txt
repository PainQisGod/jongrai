After 11 games across 3 matches yesterday, only three teams remain in the hunt. Today decides who gets to fight for the Grand Final, and the winner of that match goes on to represent Cambodia at MCT SEA in December.

## Upper Bracket Final: Vigor Apex vs Team Flash KH

Vigor Apex are the only undefeated team left this season, and they now meet Team Flash KH in the Upper Bracket Final. These are easily the two best teams of the season, and both field all-Khmer rosters of five with no imports. Expect one of the best matches of the season.

## Lower Bracket Final: the underdog waits

Waiting in the Lower Bracket Final is the underdog, TNR Esports. They opened the playoffs with a shock victory over PRO Esports, then lost to Vigor Apex and dropped into the Lower Bracket. There they beat Team RVZ, the second-placed team of the Regular Season, 3-1 to earn their place in the final. Now TNR wait for the loser of the Upper Bracket Final, hoping to keep their inspiring story going.

## The road to MCT SEA

The winners of both finals advance to the Grand Final tomorrow, and the champions will represent Cambodia at MCT SEA next month. Don't forget to come out and support your favorite team!
