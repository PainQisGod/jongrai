The long-awaited Grand Final is here! Two all-Khmer rosters are battling it out for the trophy and a seat at MCT SEA next month. Read our [Grand Final preview](/news/2026-11-15-mpl-cambodia-season-11-grand-final-preview) for the full story behind both teams.

[image: news_vgr_champion_overview.jpg | Vigor Apex, the unbeaten MPL Cambodia Season 11 champions, Photo: MPL Cambodia]

## Games 1 to 3

Vigor Apex completely dominated Game 1, playing aggressively at a tense, fast pace.

Team Flash KH answered with an early-game draft and snowballed VGR to take Game 2. It was the first game VGR dropped in the playoffs, and the first they have dropped to FLKH this season.

In Game 3, FLKH once again grabbed an early lead, but a duo setup from Shadoww's [[Alice]] and BOXI's [[Gatotkaca]] turned the tide and won the game for VGR.

## Games 4 to 6

Neither team had a clear edge early in Game 4, but after the first Lord, FLKH punished VGR. As the game went on, VGR's power spike came within reach, and with blunder after blunder from FLKH, VGR turned it around behind a Maniac from Detective to take Game 4 and reach match point.

VGR ran the same draft again with only two heroes changed, but FLKH had the better answer and took Game 5.

Finally, Detective picked [[Nolan]] in Game 6 and put on the performance of his life, sealing the victory for VGR and lifting the trophy.

[image: news_vgr_lift_trophy.jpg | Vigor Apex lift the MPL Cambodia Season 11 trophy, Photo: MPL Cambodia]

## Finals MVP

VGR's Vincen is the Finals MVP for his exceptional performance, earning two game MVPs in the series.

[image: news_vincen_final_mvp.jpg | Vincen, the Grand Final MVP, Photo: MPL Cambodia]

## Viewership

The Grand Final reached a peak of 140K concurrent viewers: 74K on the MPL Cambodia YouTube channel, 52K on its official Facebook page, and the rest across other pages and platforms.

## វគ្គទី១ ដល់វគ្គទី៣

Vigor Apex បានគ្របដណ្ដប់វគ្គទី១ទាំងស្រុង ដោយលេងយ៉ាងកាចសាហាវ ក្នុងល្បឿនលឿននិងតានតឹង។

Team Flash KH បានឆ្លើយតបដោយរៀប Draft លេងដើមវគ្គ ហើយបិទគ្រប VGR យកបានវគ្គទី២។ នេះជាវគ្គដំបូងដែល VGR ចាញ់ក្នុងវគ្គ Playoffs ហើយក៏ជាលើកដំបូងដែលចាញ់ FLKH ក្នុងរដូវកាលនេះដែរ។

នៅវគ្គទី៣ FLKH ម្ដងទៀតបាននាំមុខនៅដើមវគ្គ ប៉ុន្តែការរៀបឈុតឆាកវាយគ្នាពីដៃគូ Shadoww លើតួអង្គ [[Alice]] និង BOXI លើតួអង្គ [[Gatotkaca]] បានបង្វិលសភាពការណ៍ ហើយនាំឲ្យ VGR ឈ្នះវគ្គនេះ។

## វគ្គទី៤ ដល់វគ្គទី៦

នៅដើមវគ្គទី៤ ក្រុមទាំងពីរមិនមានអ្វីលើសគ្នាប៉ុន្មានទេ ប៉ុន្តែបន្ទាប់ពី Lord ទីមួយ FLKH បាននាំមុខ VGR។ ប៉ុន្តែពេលវេលាដើរទៅមុខ ភាពខ្លាំងរបស់ VGR ក៏មកដល់ ហើយបន្ទាប់ពី FLKH បង្កើតកំហុសជាបន្តបន្ទាប់ VGR បានបង្វិលស្ថានការណ៍ ដោយមាន Maniac ពី Detective ដើម្បីយកឈ្នះវគ្គទី៤ និងសល់តែមួយវគ្គទៀតទៅដល់ជ័យជម្នះ។

VGR បានលេង Draft ដដែលម្ដងទៀត ដោយប្ដូរតែពីរតួអង្គប៉ុណ្ណោះ ប៉ុន្តែ FLKH មានចម្លើយតបល្អជាង ហើយយកឈ្នះវគ្គទី៥។

ទីបំផុត Detective បានជ្រើសរើសតួអង្គ [[Nolan]] នៅវគ្គទី៦ ហើយបង្ហាញទម្រង់លេងដ៏អស្ចារ្យបំផុតក្នុងជីវិតរបស់គាត់ ដើម្បីជួយឲ្យ VGR យកជ័យជម្នះ និងលើកពាន។
