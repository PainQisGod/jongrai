Yesterday, TNR Esports knocked out the defending champions. Today, Vigor Apex made sure there would be no reverse sweep this time, by sweeping TNR instead.

## Games 1 and 2

Game 1 was a smooth ride for VGR. Boxi's performance on [[Marcel]] helped them snowball, and they ended it just before the 11-minute mark.

TNR fought back in the mid game of Game 2, but a Maniac from Vincen's [[Harith]], a bait play from Detective's [[Aulus]] and a brilliant setup from Shadoww's [[Barats]] secured the second game for VGR.

## Game 3

TNR went with comfort picks for every member in Game 3, while Vincen got his hands on [[Bruno]]. The early and mid game were quiet, and the team fights that followed were even, but Vincen's Bruno simply dealt too much damage for TNR's frontline to handle, closing out the sweep.

Vincen was a deserved MVP in all three games, the first player to take three MVPs in one series since Sakzuuu did it for Team Max in Season 9.

## វគ្គទី១ និងវគ្គទី២

វគ្គទី១ ចាប់ផ្ដើមដោយភាពរលូនសម្រាប់ VGR ដោយសារតែទម្រង់លេងដ៏ល្អរបស់ Boxi ទៅលើតួអង្គ [[Marcel]] ជួយពួកគេបិទខ្ទប់ ហើយបញ្ចប់វគ្គត្រឹមតែក្បែរ ១១ នាទីប៉ុណ្ណោះ។

ការយកបាន Maniac ពី Vincen ទៅលើតួអង្គ [[Harith]] ការដាក់នុយពី Detective ទៅលើ [[Aulus]] និងការរៀបចំឈុតឆាកដ៏ល្អពី Shadoww ទៅលើ [[Barats]] ជួយឲ្យ VGR យកបានវគ្គទី២ ទោះបីជាមានការព្យាយាមវាយបកពីសំណាក់ TNR នៅពាក់កណ្ដាលវគ្គ។

## វគ្គទី៣
