Throughout the season, many players showed what they can do, and seven of them stood out above the rest. Let's dive into each award, its nominees and its winner.

## Best Rookie Player

**Winner: Souur (RVZ)**

Nominees: Souur (RVZ), VBEN (RVZ), Kwayne (RVZ)

## Most Improved Player

**Winner: Vincen (VGR)**

Nominees: Vincen (VGR), Sakzuuu (FLKH), Haruki (TNR)

## Regular Season MVP

**Winner: VBEN (RVZ)**

Nominees: VBEN (RVZ), Vincen (VGR), Aries (FLKH)
