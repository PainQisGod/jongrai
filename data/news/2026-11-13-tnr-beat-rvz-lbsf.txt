Two open qualifier teams proved themselves in the regular season. But both had just lost in the Upper Bracket Semifinals this afternoon, so they got a second chance to prove themselves in the Lower Bracket Semifinal.

Team RVZ took Game 1 with aggressive gameplay, built on Virgil's wonderful setup with [[Masha]]. TNR Esports quickly denied them in the next game with a split teamfight and a backdoor play from Alfonso.

RVZ started strong in Game 3 and led by 7 thousand gold, before Tyyy's setup and a blunder from RVZ itself secured a comeback for TNR. With a draft built solely on comfort picks, TNR dominated Game 4 and ended RVZ's run.

TNR will now wait for the loser of the Upper Bracket Final in the Lower Bracket Final.
