After an amazing first day of the playoffs, the highly anticipated second day is here. TNR Esports and Team Flash KH both fought their hearts out yesterday with reverse sweeps, earning their tickets to face the two strongest teams of the season.

## Vigor Apex vs TNR Esports

Vigor Apex are still undefeated: they finished 1st in the regular season without a single series loss, and they are the strongest team on paper and the favorites to win the season. But TNR Esports can't be overlooked. They are the underdogs, and they could pull off another upset at any moment. Can Vigor Apex keep their winning streak alive, or will TNR Esports be the ones to end it?

## Team RVZ vs Team Flash KH

The other semifinal brings together two strong teams, and it should be an entertaining matchup between the best macro teams in the league right now. With a squad made up mostly of rookies, Team RVZ finished 2nd in the regular season with eight series wins, their only loss coming against Vigor Apex. Team Flash KH are still a force: four of their five players were together in Season 8 at Team Max, where they finished as runners-up. Will Team RVZ stay hungry for more, or will Team Flash KH teach them a lesson?

Day 2 of the playoffs starts at noon. Don't forget to support your favorite team!
