Draft board: a stage, not a form

The board follows the real 20-step tournament order and always says whose turn it is. Picks arrive in temple-arch doors, bans get struck through, and an edge ring tips toward the side that's winning the draft.

  • Turn rail: 20 notches (diamonds for bans, bars for picks) worked out from the board itself, so it stays right even if picks went in out of order.
  • Bans switch: turn it off for a picks-only draft in the engine's 10-step snake order, as the original board allowed. The choice is remembered.
  • Fly-in: the portrait you click arcs from the pool into its slot. Hero videos keep playing because only changed slots redraw.
  • Hover lens: hovering a pool hero tags enemy doors STRONG VS or THREAT and your own doors COMBO, using the counter and synergy data.
  • ▲/▼ counts on tiles show how many enemy picks a hero counters or is countered by; the engine's top picks get a slowly turning gold ring and their rank.
  • Hold to reset (0.7 s, or two presses from the keyboard) so a stray click never wipes a finished draft; Ctrl+Z undoes your last move.
  • Lotus profile: the nine tactical axes drawn as paired petals; coordination shows the lanes each side still needs.
  • Coach on request: Jongrai only reads when asked, so no API credit is spent by accident. Lines fade in one at a time.

Hero codex: the tier river

Instead of 133 identical cards, every hero sits in its tier band at its win rate. You can see at a glance which S-tier heroes are really coin flips, and which heroes outside the top tier are quietly winning.

  • Packing: faces nudge up and down until none overlap, and heroes fade in from left to right.
  • Coin-flip line in gold at 50%; red rings on the 14 heroes the meta bans.
  • Search dims, never hides: matches grow, the rest fade, and the river keeps its shape. Enter opens the first match.
  • Profile drawer with deep links (#ling), Esc to close, focus returned to the face you came from.
  • Weak vs is worked out by reverse lookup of every other hero's counters, so both directions of a matchup show.
  • Ratings as 10-step gauges, spike as an early/mid/late timeline, builds with core items marked by a gold diamond.

Home: the room is already alive

The home page shows the draft in progress, the meta's winners and its most-feared heroes, before you click anything.

  • Live board card: the current draft in miniature with its edge, refreshed when you come back to the tab.
  • Carved wordmark: ចង្រៃ set in Moul, a traditional Khmer display face, cut into the page rather than glowing on it.
  • Key art drifts over 40 s; embers rise and pause while the tab is hidden.
  • Counters roll up when the stats strip scrolls into view; the ban bars grow in turn.
  • Tip carousel from the heroes' own tips, with a progress bar that pauses on hover.
